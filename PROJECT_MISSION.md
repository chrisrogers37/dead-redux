# Dead Redux: Mission

**Dead Redux is an immersive world, and no two days in it are the same: each brings a different set, a different setting and a different cast of characters.**

That is the target, however far off, and every idea and feature lines up under it. The way there starts from what the site already is: one Grateful Dead show a day, played in a 3D world built from that show's setlist.

## How the world behaves

- **Every day is somewhere new.** Whoever comes back tomorrow finds a different setting and different characters, not just a different show.
- **The show drives it.** The day's setlist and its sound shape everything in the world: its land, its light and its characters.
- **Rich, not basic.** Every setting has the detail, the light and the life to be worth stepping into.
- **Its characters are at the show too.** Whoever the day brings, on stage or off, moves to the music and takes notice of the visitor.
- **The visitor is in it, not watching it.** They move through the world as one of its characters, free to join the others or to lean back and take it all in.

## Who it's for

- **Anyone who comes for the day's show.** Everyone who comes that day finds the same set, the same setting and the same characters.
- **The next day brings them back:** a different show to hear and a different place to be.
- **On whatever they come with.** Every device gets the world, scaled to it, and a visitor who asks for reduced motion, or whose browser can't draw 3D, still gets the day's show.

## The test

Ask three questions of any idea or feature:

1. Does it make the world more immersive, or its days more different from one another?
2. Does it keep the day's show at the center?
3. Does everyone who comes for the show still get it?

It fits when all three answers are yes.
